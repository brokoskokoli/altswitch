Privacy Policy

Applies to all versions of AltSwitch. Last updated: October 18, 2026.

In short: AltSwitch does not collect, store or share any personal data, and it never connects to the internet. Everything it sees of your windows stays on your Mac.

No data collection

AltSwitch has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no user accounts. The developers receive no information about you or how you use the app.

No network connections

AltSwitch makes no network connections at all – it does not even check for updates. Updates come from GitHub Releases or Homebrew, when you choose to install them.

What stays on your computer

  • Window list and previews. To show its cards, AltSwitch reads the titles and positions of your open windows and, with the Screen Recording permission, takes small preview images of them. These are kept in memory only while the app runs, are never written to disk and never leave your Mac.
  • Settings. Your choices (shortcut, layout, whether setup is done) are stored in ~/Library/Preferences/com.stefanrichter.altswitch.plist.
  • Start at login is registered with macOS (System Settings → General → Login Items) and can be turned off there or in the AltSwitch menu.

Permissions

  • Accessibility – to catch the keyboard shortcut and bring the window you pick to the front.
  • Screen Recording (optional) – only for the preview images. Without it, AltSwitch shows app icons instead.

You can revoke both at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit it; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
