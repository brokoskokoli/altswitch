AltSwitch

Windows-style Alt-Tab for your Mac. Switch windows, not just apps.

AltSwitch shows every open window of every app as a card with a live preview. Hold ⌥ or ⌘, tap Tab, let go – and the window you picked is in front.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Signed and notarized · Free and open source (MIT)

Why AltSwitch?

The built-in ⌘Tab switches between apps. With three browser windows, two documents and a terminal open, you still end up in Mission Control. AltSwitch lists each window on its own, most recently used first – like Alt-Tab on Windows.

⌘-Tab is fast and always there – great when you only need to change apps. AltSwitch is for when you want a particular window.

Every window, one keystroke away

A small, native menu bar app that does one thing well.

🪟

Window by window

Every open window of every app is its own card, in most-recently-used order. The window you used before is selected on the first Tab.

🖼️

Live previews

Each card shows a current image of the window, so you recognise the right document at a glance.

📐

Two layouts

A compact carousel for quick switches, or a grid of all windows – like Task View – with arrow-key navigation in all four directions.

⌨️

Your shortcut

Use ⌥Tab or replace ⌘Tab. Go back with ⇧Tab or a tap on ⇧, cancel with Esc.

🖱️

One click

Click a card and its window comes to the front – the first click counts, no double click.

🔽

Minimized windows too

Minimized windows appear with a badge and are restored when you pick them.

⚙️

Lives in the menu bar, no Dock icon. A setup assistant walks you through the permissions; start at login is one click.

Written in Swift with AppKit and SwiftUI. No Electron, no background services. Universal for Apple silicon and Intel.

Private

Install

  1. Download the DMG from the releases page.
  2. Open it and drag AltSwitch into Applications. Releases are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
  3. Start AltSwitch. The setup assistant asks for Accessibility (needed to catch the shortcut and focus windows) and, optionally, Screen Recording (only for the previews – without it, cards show the app icon).

Or with Homebrew: brew install --cask brokoskokoli/tap/altswitch

Requirements

macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

Build from source

git clone https://github.com/brokoskokoli/altswitch.git, then ./build.sh builds .build/AltSwitch.app. Details in the README.

FAQ

Is AltSwitch really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license, with no ads, no subscription and no account.

Does AltSwitch connect to the internet?

No. It makes no network connections at all. Details in the privacy policy.

Why does it need Accessibility and Screen Recording?

Accessibility lets AltSwitch catch ⌥Tab or ⌘Tab and bring a specific window to the front. Screen Recording is only used for the preview images of your windows; they never leave your Mac. Without it, AltSwitch still works and shows app icons instead.

Can it replace ⌘-Tab?

Yes. Choose Command + Tab in the menu bar menu. You can switch back to ⌥Tab at any time.

How do I update?

With Homebrew: brew upgrade --cask altswitch. Otherwise download the new DMG from the releases page and replace the app.

Are there other window switchers?

Yes, for example the open-source AltTab, which has many more options. AltSwitch aims to be small and simple.
